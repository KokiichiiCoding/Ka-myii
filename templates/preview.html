Body & physics

Springy nod adds a head dip and rebound with quieter shoulders and trailing hair. Head sway tilts the head side to side at half the nod speed while the neck bends and the shoulders stay put. 0 turns either off; camera mode follows your own head instead.

Hair and body follow through after a turn. Physics must be on; Idle adds movement while you are still.

Natural sway shifts weight through the hips, pauses, then settles on the other side. Feet stay planted. Weight shift and breathing require Idle; camera mode follows your own head.

Body motion adds a torso arc, shoulder roll and breathing. The torso follows your head with a short delay; the lower body stays planted. Body turn and Body tilt also work as manual poses.

No arms or legs in the picture? Complete the body ↗ paints them in as a new character to separate.

Earrings, tails, hats and bows. A hat's brim and tip trail the head; the part on the head stays put.

Nekomimi & tail

Fit animated cat ears and a tail to any character. The ears twitch and the tail swishes with Idle; both react to turns with Physics on.

Save adjustments to keep the fit. These additions appear in preview PNGs and recordings; the artist PSD and Cubism package keep the original artwork. Turn ears off when the character already has them.
