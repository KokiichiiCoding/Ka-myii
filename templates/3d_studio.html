Motion & expression

Choose the mouth shape for movement and face tracking.

Keys 1–6 play them too, Shift+1–9 switch expressions and M the microphone. They work with the camera on.

Spring physics on the chest; Hop shows it best. VSeeFace and other VRM apps play the 50% setting.

A tail needs a full-body avatar.

Ears twitch and lag head turns; the tail sways and swings with the hips. They show on the stage, in pictures and clips, not in the downloaded VRM.

Expressions and motion depend on the avatar’s rig. Ka-myii avatars blink and talk with the character’s own painted eyes and mouth when it was separated into layers. The upper body gently follows the head; it is not full-body tracking.
